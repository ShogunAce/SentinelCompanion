Sentinel

Is your app leaking anything it shouldn't?

Paste your app's code and Sentinel will spot the most common security slip-ups in apps made with Lovable, Bolt, Replit, or AI Studio, then tell you how to fix them.

Sentinel asks a free public relay service to download your app's public page, since browsers can't do that directly. Your address is shared with that relay. Pasting your code works more reliably.

Your code stays in this browser. The only thing sent out is a short list of the problems found (with any keys partly hidden), which goes to Google's Gemini AI so it can explain them in plain English.
